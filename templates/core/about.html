The SKuL Mission • About Live School

Education Built For Everyone.

SKuL (originally LiveSchool) is a next-generation open academy built on the firm conviction that high-level education in computing, science, engineering, and digital craft should belong to humanity freely — without paywalls, fees, or geographical barriers.

Zero Tuition Gatekeeping

Every course, syllabus, quiz, and study resource is fully accessible to any student worldwide with no credit card required.

Cross-Demographic Inclusion

Designed to bridge educational inequalities with sensory accessibility, dyslexia toggles, lightweight notes, and offline printability.

Cryptographic Distinction

Rigorous course evaluations that culminate in verifiable, digitally signed completion certificates with tamper-evident serials.
