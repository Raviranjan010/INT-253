About Live School

Empowering humanity through democratized access to world-class learning, synchronous live teaching, and verified credentials.

ACADEMIC PROBLEM STATEMENT

"Live School for World-Class Free Education: Creation of an online platform providing free access to world-class education for students across various demographics."

1. Background & Motivation

Across the globe, billions of ambitious minds are restrained from realizing their full human potential due to exorbitant university tuitions, prohibitive paywalls on learning resources, geographical isolation, and social divides. Traditional Massive Open Online Courses (MOOCs) often monetize certificates or lack the dynamic mentorship and real-time interaction that students in developing or underserved demographics desperately need.

2. The Solution: Live School

Live School bridges the gap between static self-paced tutorials and expensive private tutoring. Built as an open, accessible Django-powered web ecosystem, Live School combines:

  • Open-Access Curriculum: Structured courses spanning Computer Science, Mathematics, STEM, Languages, and Essential Life Skills.
  • Live Interactive Video Rooms: Real-time zero-cost live classes with screen sharing, teacher-student dialogue, and doubt-clearing sessions using WebRTC.
  • Demographics-Centric Accessibility: Built-in text resizing, high-contrast modes, dyslexia-friendly fonts, low-bandwidth video integration, and demographic tracking.
  • Verifiable Digital Credentials: Instant cryptographically verifiable completion certificates with SHA-256 validation for students to showcase on resumes and professional profiles.
