UN SDG 4 Framework • Global Demographic Inclusivity

Education for Every Demographic

How Live School dismantles financial, infrastructural, and linguistic divides to deliver dignified, barrier-free higher learning to underserved communities worldwide.

🌾 Rural & Remote Learners

Engineered to function reliably on 2G/3G connections. Lecture notes and reading modules are lightweight text and downloadable files, ensuring learning never stops even with intermittent electricity or unstable network connections.

  • Low-bandwidth adaptive stream
  • Lightweight downloadable Markdown resources
  • Asynchronous discussion forums

💰 Economically Disadvantaged

Eliminates all tuition, subscription paywalls, and pay-to-receive certificate schemes. Every learner receives world-class instruction, assessments, and verified credentials completely gratis.

  • Zero registration or hidden fees
  • Free tamper-evident digital certificates with SHA-256
  • No commercial advertising friction

👩‍💻 Women & Girls in STEM

Provides a safe, respectful, and inclusive online environment with female instructors and peer mentorship groups encouraging active participation in Computer Science, Data, and Mathematics.

  • Curated STEM courses with inclusive mentorship
  • Safe moderated live classrooms and doubt boards
  • Community support circles

💼 First-Gen & Working Adults

Adults balancing family and wage labor require extreme flexibility. Courses are structured into bite-sized 10-15 minute modular lessons with evening and weekend live classes.

  • Self-paced modular curriculum
  • Evening live masterclasses and recordings
  • Gamified daily streak and XP tracking

♿ Differently-Abled Learners

Features an instant accessibility toolbar allowing students to switch between High-Contrast vision modes, adjust font sizes on the fly, use dyslexia-friendly fonts, and hear text-to-speech narration.

  • High-contrast visual accessibility switch
  • Real-time browser speech synthesis audio reader
  • Keyboard-navigable semantic HTML

🌐 Global South Students

Open worldwide to students across Africa, Asia, Latin America, and beyond. Cross-border peer forums facilitate knowledge exchange between diverse cultural perspectives.

  • Universal web standards compatibility
  • Global peer Q&A discussion exchange
  • Demographic-based impact measurement
